Obstacle detection pipeline
===========================

Input: final FUSED BUFFER 0 (up to 30,000 points).

1. Ground RANSAC
   - 48 deterministic RANSAC candidates.
   - plane inlier tolerance: 5 cm.
   - ground normal must keep |nz| >= 0.60, so walls are rejected.
   - best plane is refined with least-squares z = ax + by + c.
   - plane normal is flipped toward the LiDAR origin, therefore signed
     distance from the plane is also approximate obstacle height.

2. Candidate filter
   - horizontal range: 0.12 .. 12 m.
   - signed height over ground: 0.05 .. 2.50 m.

3. Clustering
   - project candidates to 15 cm XY cells.
   - connect 8-neighbor occupied cells.
   - minimum 8 points per object.
   - a one-cell object needs at least 20 points.
   - keep at most the nearest 64 objects.

4. Output
   Each object contains center XYZ, XYZ bounding-box size, shortest horizontal
   distance to the box, point count and flags. Objects are sorted nearest first.

Tuning constants are at the top of lidar_obstacles.c.
